# Living rules and readiness

Develop the maintained player rulebook, readable by teenagers and future players. Lead with shared survival and individual goals, then limits, turn options, crisis refills, decisions, weighted voting, reviews and round-end completion. Illustrate these rules with CSV-linked cards through the same Board used in the overview.

Each structured section separates player prose from development reasoning, questions and TODOs. A development-only toggle reveals annotations; production shows neither toggle nor notes. Actual missing player rules remain explicit in both modes. Readiness counts missing content by category, not a misleading completion percentage. Collect view metadata through the registry without duplicating checklists. Preserve the exact unresolved mechanics from discovery beside the rules they affect.
