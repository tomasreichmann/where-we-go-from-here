# Component styleguide

Let contributors choose and extend presentational pieces before composing views. Start with semantic tokens and typography, then a complete “use when” index, primitives, cards, outcomes/orientation, identity/influence, scales and static Board layouts. Demonstrate realistic source records in prompt-only, partial, authored, long-prose, A-selected, B-selected, ongoing-crisis and permanent-policy states.

Canonical geometry must scale as a whole; readable documents grow with text. Test color-independent labels, overlapping national limits, weighted aspects and rotation bounds. Fan/deck overlap belongs to non-active material; keep active outcomes exposed. The provisional Climate treatment, taxonomy and capstone requirements stay visibly provisional. Record remaining local tasks in view.meta.ts.
